# Vision and scope

## Problem statement

Students may accumulate achievements, projects, leadership, service, certifications, and work experience across many places. They may later struggle to retrieve evidence or explain specific contributions when requesting a recommendation. This is a problem hypothesis for elicitation, not a claim that current university systems are defective.

## Vision

AU Student Profile helps students preserve and organize evidence-backed achievements, understand verification status, prepare useful recommendation evidence packs, and make deliberate sharing choices.

## Users and goals

Primary users are students and alumni. Supporting users include lecturers/supervisors, Career Services, academic administration, Alumni Office, external recipients, and ICT/security teams. Goals are to improve evidence organization, make review explicit, reduce recommender context-gathering, and make privacy choices understandable.

## Scope for this prototype

The prototype includes a student dashboard, editable profile, achievement lifecycle, simulated reviewer queue, recommendation requests, evidence-pack print view, privacy preview, activity history, settings, and synthetic demo roles.

## Non-goals and boundaries

It does not replace the AU portal, perform authentication, send email, upload real evidence, issue official recommendations, provide public sharing links, or enforce production authorization. No integration with AU systems is assumed.

## Initial assumptions and constraints

The first version uses fictional records and in-memory React state. Stakeholders may disagree about evidence standards, verifier authority, retention, and audience definitions. The workshop must validate those rules before implementation is treated as an SRS.

## Success criteria

Workshop participants can follow a coherent create → submit → review → share journey; distinguish claims from verified records; understand what an external recipient sees; and identify unanswered business and security questions.
