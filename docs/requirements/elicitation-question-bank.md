# Elicitation question bank

## Evidence and verification

- What should a student own, and what may a department amend?
- Which evidence types are acceptable for academic, service, leadership, and work achievements?
- Who is authorized to verify each category? What happens if the verifier has a conflict?
- What is the difference between rejected and changes requested? Is appeal required?
- May a student resubmit indefinitely, and what feedback is mandatory?

## Recommendations and access

- What information does a recommender actually need, and what should stay private?
- How should an institution, employer, or alumni recipient receive consented evidence?
- Should a request expire, be revoked, or be copied into an official process?
- What access should alumni retain after graduation?

## Privacy, accessibility, and operations

- Which sections default to only-me, and which may be visible to Career Services?
- What consent and revocation language is needed for external sharing?
- What accessibility and low-bandwidth constraints are common for students?
- Who owns retention, correction, deletion, and incident response?
- What notifications are useful without creating pressure or revealing sensitive content?

## Integration and success

- Is single sign-on with the existing portal a hypothesis, and who must approve it?
- Which records, if any, should be sourced from institutional systems?
- What outcome would demonstrate value: completion, recommendation turnaround, student confidence, or employment support?
- What exceptions or conflicting expectations would make the workflow fail?

## Identity and institutional integration

- Is the proposed six-digit `YYNNNN` student/staff identifier format correct?
- Do all relevant staff categories use the same format?
- How is an account's role authoritatively determined, and who owns that mapping?
- Are students, lecturers, and administrators managed through the same identity provider?
- Does AU ICT offer an approved SSO integration for external applications?
- What authentication standards and policies must developers follow?
- How should alumni access be managed after graduation?
- How should external recipients access specifically shared portfolios without an AU staff ID?
- Who is authorized to grant, review, and revoke permissions?
