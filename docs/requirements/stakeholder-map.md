# Stakeholder map

| Stakeholder | Interest / responsibility | Information needs | Concerns |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student | Owns profile and chooses submissions/sharing | Status, evidence, feedback, audiences | Effort, privacy, unfair rejection |
| Alumni | Maintains a durable portfolio after graduation | Retention, access, export | Account continuity, data ownership |
| Lecturer / Supervisor | Reviews evidence and may recommend | Context, evidence, decision history | Workload, conflicts of interest |
| Academic administrator | May define academic records/processes | Policy, exceptions, reporting | Governance and accuracy |
| Career Services | Supports employability and referrals | Verified skills, consent, career context | Appropriate access and stale records |
| Alumni Office | Supports post-graduation engagement | Opt-in outcomes and contact rules | Consent and retention |
| External recipient | Reviews a specifically shared portfolio | Clear, bounded evidence | Authenticity and over-sharing |
| ICT/security team | Owns secure operation and integration advice | Threat model, identity, audit, retention | Authorization, incident response, integration risk |

Authority is not equal: students control their submissions and sharing choices; authorized institutional reviewers make verification decisions; ICT/security defines production controls; external recipients do not become system users merely because a portfolio is shared.

## Initial responsibility matrix

| Activity | Student | Reviewer | Career Services | ICT/security |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Create/edit achievement | R | C | I | C |
| Verify evidence | I | A/R | C | I |
| Choose sharing audience | A/R | C | C | C |
| Operate identity and authorization | I | I | I | A/R |
| Preserve audit trail | I | C | C | A/R |

R = responsible, A = accountable, C = consulted, I = informed. Validate this matrix in the workshop.
